TurnScene

Privacy Policy

TurnScene 1.1 · Updated November 4, 2026

TurnScene’s photography tools keep your photos, captions, drafts, and creations on your device without automatic upload and do not require an account. The app also uses online pages, installation measurement, sign-in, and notification services that process information off your device. Local photo storage does not mean that the app sends no data.

1. Scope

This policy explains how TurnScene (the “app”) and its developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handle information when you use the app or contact support. The app includes photography tasks, photo capture and import, cropping, captions, local creation management, export and sharing, and online services. Depending on the destination selected during setup, the app can open a remotely hosted page. Information you provide on an online page is separate from locally saved photo creations; review any additional notices shown by that service.

2. Information We Process and Why

  • Photos and creative content: Photos you take or import into the photography tools, captions, task records, crop areas, layout choices, drafts, completion status, and modification timestamps are used to edit, save, display, and export your creations. This content is stored locally. This statement does not cover content you choose to submit to online pages or share with other services.
  • Preferences: Haptic feedback, sound, motion, and the native notification-switch preference are stored on your device to remember your choices. The app also remembers the online destination returned during setup.
  • Cache and temporary files: The app generates photo thumbnails and temporary export files to improve browsing performance and enable sharing.
  • Installation and app-use measurement: AppsFlyer is used for installation attribution and session measurement. Our separate first-open service at attributionsolutions.com receives an app installation identifier, the AppsFlyer identifier, the identifier for vendor (IDFV) when available, browser user-agent information, and available attribution fields. The service can return the online destination shown by the app.
  • Sign-in: When you use Google sign-in on an online page, Google processes the account information needed to authenticate you. The app can exchange Google credentials with Firebase Authentication and return a Google access token or Firebase identity token to the online service to complete sign-in.
  • Notifications: Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging use device or installation tokens to register the app for notification delivery. The app can provide its FCM token to the online page. Registration and associated service processing can occur separately from permission to display alerts, sounds, and badges.
  • Service information: Integrated services can process device and installation identifiers, app activity, operating-system and app information, network information, and diagnostics. For example, Google sign-in can use an IP address to estimate a general location for fraud prevention. These services are not limited to launches that show an online page.
  • Support emails: If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments, device details, or operating system information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond and provide support. Please share only what is necessary to resolve your request.

The photography tools do not require sign-in. Online features can use Google and Firebase authentication. Selecting “Indoors” or “Outdoors” for a photo task is a manual choice and does not use your device location.

3. Permissions and Your Choices

Camera: The app requests camera permission only when you choose to take a photo and permission has not yet been granted. Camera access is used to capture photos for your creations. If you decline, you can still import photos and use other available features. You can change TurnScene’s camera permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Photo import: The app uses the system photo picker and receives only the images you select. It does not request access to your entire photo library. The app stores local copies of selected photos; editing or deleting these copies does not change the originals in your system photo library.

If a selected photo is stored in iCloud, the system may need to download it through Apple’s services before making it available to the app. This processing is governed by your system settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

Tracking: The app requests App Tracking Transparency permission during startup when your choice is undetermined and iOS permits the prompt. You can allow or decline and review your choice in iOS Settings. Installation and first-open measurement run independently of this prompt and can send information before you respond. The app continues loading its destination while the prompt is shown. Declining does not disable all data processing: installation measurement, notification registration, and sign-in can still exchange information with online services.

Notifications: Turning on Daily Recommendations in the native Settings screen requests system notification permission if you have not already decided. The online experience can also request this permission. Turning the native switch off only saves that preference; it does not revoke permission, stop existing notifications, or delete registration tokens. The switch does not schedule daily local reminders. Use iOS Settings to control notification delivery.

Location: The online experience can request permission to use your location while the app is in use. The native photography tools do not need device location. You can decline or change location access in iOS Settings. The original photos’ location metadata is not included in JPGs exported by the photography tools.

4. Sharing, Third Parties, and Disclosure

The photography tools do not automatically upload your locally stored creative content. Attribution, sign-in, and notification services do exchange information as described above; those exchanges should not be confused with photo storage.

AppsFlyer, Google, Firebase, Apple, and the online services you use process information needed for their respective services. Details about provider practices are available in AppsFlyer’s customer data information, Google’s privacy policy, and Firebase’s privacy information. These services may process information in locations outside your country.

When you choose to use the system sharing feature, the recipient, app, or service you select receives the exported files and handles them under its own privacy policy. Exported images include your artwork and captions but do not include the original photos’ GPS location metadata. The visible images or text may still contain personal information you choose to include.

When you contact us by email, email service providers process the transmission and storage of your messages. We use this information to handle support requests and may disclose information we actually hold when necessary to meet applicable legal requirements.

5. Storage, Backups, and Retention

Photos, drafts, creations, and preferences used by the photography tools are stored on your device until you delete the relevant content or delete the app. These tools do not automatically synchronize your creations to a cloud account.

Cloud Sync & Backup uses Google sign-in, verified by Firebase Authentication. The separate native Apple sign-in flow handles the system authorization result locally, displays a success message, and stores your Apple sign-in identifier on this device to remember your sign-in. Signing out or deleting the Apple sign-in clears this local state. It does not request your name or email, send Apple credentials to Firebase, create a cloud account, or upload a backup. Your account identifier and any name or email provided are used to authenticate you and restrict backup access. When you tap Back Up Now, your works, drafts, photos, captions, crop areas, layouts, and editing relationships are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage under your account. Backups do not include sign-in credentials, notification tokens, or device permissions. They are snapshots; subsequent edits do not sync automatically. Restoration adds new local copies without replacing existing work. You can delete individual cloud backups or use Delete Account & Cloud Data to remove your backups and Firebase sign-in account after Google identity verification. Copies already restored on devices remain local. Requests involving information held by other online services can be made using the contact address in this policy.

Depending on your device settings, local app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. The relevant system or service manages backup storage, restoration, and deletion. We cannot access or delete these backups on your behalf.

We retain support emails only for as long as needed to handle your request, conduct necessary follow-up, and meet applicable legal obligations, after which we delete or anonymize them. You may request deletion using the contact email in this policy.

Online accounts, attribution records, and notification registration information are separate from local creations. Deleting a local creation, clearing the photo cache, or deleting the app does not automatically erase information already held by online services. Contact us for information about retention or deletion of records associated with your use of TurnScene; provider-specific retention practices are also described in their privacy information.

6. Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • Delete creations: Delete a creation from its detail page. Associated photo copies that are no longer used by other creations or drafts are also removed.
  • Delete drafts: Go to Settings → Storage to delete unwanted drafts.
  • Clear the photo cache: Select Clear Photo Cache in Storage. This preserves your photos, drafts, and creations. It does not clear website cookies, online accounts, or service records.
  • Export content: Export a creation or use Settings → Export Work to save images of your work. Exported images do not include the complete draft data needed to restore an editable creation.
  • Delete app data: Use Delete App in iOS to remove the app and its local data. Offload App may retain documents and data. Copies you have shared, exported, or backed up must be deleted separately from their respective locations.
  • Permissions: Review camera, location, notification, and tracking choices in iOS Settings. Changing permission does not automatically delete information previously sent to a service.
  • Privacy requests: Contact us with questions or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of TurnScene, including support messages or online services, or to exercise other rights available under applicable law. We may need to take reasonable steps to verify your identity to protect your information.

Because we cannot access creations stored on your device, we cannot remotely read, restore, or delete them in response to an email. You can manage this content directly using the app and system features described above.

7. Information Security

The app uses the iOS app sandbox and system permissions to restrict other apps’ access to local data. We take reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no device or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device passcode and choose sharing recipients carefully.

8. Children’s Privacy

TurnScene is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should review online services and permissions as well as any photos or messages a child shares. If you believe a child has provided information through TurnScene that should be deleted, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy as app features or applicable requirements change and publish the revised content on this page. For changes that materially affect how information is handled, we will notify you through an in-app notice or another appropriate method. Where additional consent is required, we will obtain it before the relevant processing begins.
